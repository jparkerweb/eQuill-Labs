Shrinkray is a cross-platform CLI video compression tool powered by FFmpeg, featuring a wizard-style TUI built with Go and Charm's Bubble Tea. The interactive wizard gives step-by-step guided compression with real-time preview, and ships 18 built-in presets spanning quality tiers, purpose-driven profiles and platform-specific targets such as Discord, YouTube and TikTok. It analyzes a video and suggests the optimal preset, and auto-detects hardware acceleration across NVIDIA NVENC, AMD AMF, Apple VideoToolbox and Intel QSV. Entire folders can be compressed with parallel workers and progress tracking, and a headless `--no-tui` mode with stdin pipe support makes it scriptable for CI/CD pipelines. It ships as a single static binary for Windows, macOS and Linux.
